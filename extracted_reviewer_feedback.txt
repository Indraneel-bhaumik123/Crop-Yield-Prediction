8. Section 6.5 — Random vs Temporal Validation
Excellent idea but correct the interpretation.

Better scientific interpretation:
Random splitting yielded substantially more optimistic performance than chronological evaluation, indicating that conventional random partitioning may underestimate generalization error in temporally ordered agricultural yield data.
That is safer and more defensible.
